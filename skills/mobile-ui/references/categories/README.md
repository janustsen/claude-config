# Category playbooks (iPhone)

The laws apply everywhere, but each category has one question users judge it on. Find the category, read its chapter, and give the richest components to that question. Most real apps mix two or three: a business app like an internal dashboard is tasks + mail + glance dashboard + notes.

| Category | Users judge it on | The richest components go to | Restraint goes to | Chapter |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tasks and to-dos | Can I capture in seconds and clear my day with my thumb? | Quick add with parsing, drag-to-place, the tick and its beat, Today | Fields, settings, labels on rows | [tasks.md](tasks.md) |
| Notes and documents | Is my writing comfortable, and can I find my way through pages? | The editor's type and margins, the keyboard toolbar, the page tree, the database list view | Chrome over the text; drag handles | [notes-and-documents.md](notes-and-documents.md) |
| AI assistants | Can I ask, read and steer with one thumb? | The composer's bottom-right slot, streaming that keeps still, voice, approvals in place | Model jargon, chrome around answers | [ai-assistants.md](ai-assistants.md) |
| Mail and CRM | Can I triage fast and find the person or record? | Row anatomy, the swipe pair, grouping machine mail, the record header and timeline | Folders, labels, settings | [mail-and-crm.md](mail-and-crm.md) |
| Calendar and time | What is next, and where is my free time? | The density strip or grid, the now line, sentence entry, the Join action | Week numbers, decoration | [calendar-and-time.md](calendar-and-time.md) |
| Glance dashboards and system health | Do I need to act? | The hero verdict, inserted alert modules, uniform tiles | Tiles that drive no decision | [glance-dashboards.md](glance-dashboards.md) |
| Money | Can I trust this number, and is this commit safe? | The hero number, the scrubbable chart, the commit facts, the receipt | Colour on rows, celebration | [money.md](money.md) |
| Live status and maps | What is happening, and what do I do next? | The phase-driven status card, the persistent sheet, the Live Activity | Static detail | [live-status-and-maps.md](live-status-and-maps.md) |
| Health and habit | Am I okay today, and did I do it? | The score with its sentence, the personal baseline, deliberate completion | Red, shame, per-tap celebration | [health-and-habit.md](health-and-habit.md) |
| Media, social and camera | Does the content own the screen, and do controls fall under my thumb? | The player or camera, the feed item, the message bubble | UI chrome over media | [media-social-camera.md](media-social-camera.md) |

## Emotional register by category

| Dimension | Tasks, notes, mail | Glance, live status | Money | Health and habit | Media, social |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| First job | Get out of the way, make capture instant | Answer "act or not" in one look | Make every number trustworthy | Lower anxiety, then help | Let the content fill the screen |
| Colour | Identity colours per list or project; one tint | State colour on live values only; background encodes overall state | Direction only, on the change line and chart | Category colours as labels; no alarm red | Media brings the colour; UI monochrome |
| Density | High: 44-50pt task rows, 79-102pt mail rows | One hero plus modules | Medium: rows grouped by date | Low: one metric per card | Content-sized |
| Motion | Fast pickers (about 130ms), a completion beat | Silent in-place updates; one live marker | Almost none; a rolling digit | Gentle; one earned celebration | Rich transitions (player expand, zoom) |
| Copy | Terse verbs | Verdict + cause | Neutral, exact | Warm, first person, baseline words | The user's voice |
